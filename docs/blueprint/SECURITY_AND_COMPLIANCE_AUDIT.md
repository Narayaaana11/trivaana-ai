## 2. Indian Telecom & Regulatory Compliance Audit (TRAI TCCCPR / DLT / DPDP Act 2023)

### 2.1 What Exists Today
* IST calling-window gate (`10:00`–`18:00` IST) + weekday filter (`ComplianceService.is_within_calling_hours`).
* Workspace-level DNC table (`do_not_call_entries`), lead `opt_out` flag, and `consent_records` table (`ComplianceService.is_suppressed`).
* 24-hour per-number outbound cap (`max_per_day=3` in `ComplianceService.check_daily_call_cap`).
* Recording disclosure flag (`recording_disclosure_enabled`) and greeting prompt template.

### 2.2 Critical Regulatory Gaps Before Live Commercial Telemarketing
1. **TRAI TCCCPR / DLT Registration Verification**: No storage or verification of Principal Entity (PE) ID, Telemarketer (TM) ID, Header/140-series (promotional) vs 160-series (service/transactional) allocation, or DLT scrubbing integration against national TRAI NCPR/DND preference registries (currently only checks workspace-local `do_not_call_entries`).
2. **Real-Time Opt-Out Enforcement**: `AIPromptService.simulate_agent_turn` detects `opt_out_detected` in text, and `RealtimeVoicePipeline` returns the boolean flag—**however, nothing in `RealtimeVoicePipeline` or `TelephonyStreamSession` automatically persists the number into `do_not_call_entries` or terminates the call when opt-out is detected.**
3. **DPDP Act 2023 (India Digital Personal Data Protection Act)**:
   * **Consent Artefact Traceability**: Consent records lack IP/timestamp/form-checksum/purpose-specific granularity and revocation webhook notifications.
   * **Data Retention & Erasure**: No automated TTL purging for call recordings, transcripts, or caller memories; no self-service "Right to Erasure / Data Export" workflow (`Settings -> Privacy & Data` only lists audit logs and caller memories).
4. **Zero-Cost Billing Safeguards**: Adding phone numbers and concurrent channels (`phone_numbers.py`) does not bill `NUMBER_RENTAL_PAISE_PER_MONTH` or `CHANNEL_RENTAL_PAISE_PER_MONTH`, and `CallService` always bills at standard rate (`650` paise/min) ignoring premium voice tiers (`850` paise/min) and Super Admin `platform_settings` overrides.

## 3. Compliance & Security Acceptance Criteria for Production Launch
1. `SEC-01` through `SEC-09` closed with automated regression tests in `backend/tests/` running against both SQLite and PostgreSQL.
2. Live Razorpay webhook + verification idempotency tested with simulated mid-transaction failures confirming zero state-commit mismatch.
3. All tenant & platform secrets encrypted at rest with rotation support; `/api/media/*` verified safe against traversal (`..`) and cross-tenant reads.
4. Automated opt-out persistence wired directly into live voice turn execution + DLT/PE/header metadata mandatory before activating non-sandbox promotional campaigns.
